# Radioactive Co-Speakers

Working for a big company is a blessing in many ways, but it sets a really high
bar for conference speaking. KubeCon talk submissions have a company diversity
requirement — your proposal is compared against all other submissions and then
cut based on a per-company "quota".

So your submission has to be exceptionally good just to make it through. This
makes Googlers and other big-company employees "radioactive" as co-speakers —
teaming up with one means your talk now competes inside that company's
allocation too.

It's a fascinating challenge though. The bar being higher makes the acceptance
that much more interesting.
