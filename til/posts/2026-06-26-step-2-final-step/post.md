# "Step 2": add a snappy title

I've been noticing a pattern for a while, but today it really hit me — more and more
PRs and CLs are showing up with titles like "step 2" or "final step".

These are clearly leftovers from AI-generated plans or skills execution. The tool breaks work into steps,
the author accepts the plan, and the step name leaks straight into the PR title
without a second thought.

Whenever I see it - I have an itch to ask for the full plan and let me talk to your AI.
Not sure if authors keep this intentionally though...
